# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Fixed

- During ARRL Field Day, GOTA contacts earned their 5-point bonus but were left out of QSO points on **Scoring**, the event dashboard, **Logbook**, and the ARRL submission sheet. They now earn QSO points by mode with the power multiplier applied, plus the unchanged GOTA bonus. Upgrading automatically rescores existing events with GOTA contacts, raising affected Field Day scores.
- On **Scoring** and the event dashboard, the band/mode tables now show GOTA contacts on their own **GOTA** row. The ARRL submission sheet's **Band/Mode QSO Breakdown** also includes a **GOTA** row instead of telling you to leave those contacts out.
- On **Logbook**, the GOTA bonus line below **QSO Points** could show the wrong total for contacts brought into the log. It now uses the same contact count times 5 as **Scoring**.
- The dashboard's **Bonus Points** tile and bonus progress bar could disagree with **Scoring** because they included unverified claims and missed bonuses earned from the log, including emergency power, youth participation, and satellite QSOs. Both now show the same verified bonus total as **Scoring**.
- The ARRL submission sheet's **Bonus Points** list included bonuses the entry class couldn't earn, such as Site Responsibilities for Class A, and a GOTA bonus line without a GOTA station. Unclaimed bonuses that don't apply are now left off.
- The ARRL submission sheet's **Participants** count left out volunteers who worked a shift but didn't log contacts or operate GOTA. It now includes those volunteers, with no-shows excluded, as the volunteer hours report does.
- On **Start Session**, picking a band the radio didn't cover showed a warning whose supported-band list could include bands missing from **Station supports** above it. The warning now identifies its list as the radio's coverage, while **Station supports** lists bands covered by both the radio and an antenna.
- On screens 1024–1279px wide with the desktop sidebar showing, especially when expanded, the header's event name wrapped and crowded the title. On **My Dashboard**, three narrow columns left the **Time Remaining** clock overflowing, milestone counts cut off, and **Sections Worked** bars reduced to dots. **Select Station** also cut off station names. The countdown now hides the repeated event name below 1280px and keeps **Ends in** on one line. Dashboard and station cards now use two columns with the sidebar expanded, and the clock sizes itself to fit. On touchscreens, the clipped **Log Contact** sub-mode placeholder also has a wider box.

- On **Log Contact**, recalling a contact with the up arrow and pressing Enter with an exchange the server rejected silently closed the edit. Typing again logged a new contact. The edit now stays open with the error shown, and a connection failure says the edit wasn't saved.
- On **Transcribe Paper Log**, contact times ahead of the clock were accepted. Times more than five minutes ahead are now refused. An unreadable leading time, such as `2575 W1AW 3A CT`, now reports an invalid time format instead of asking for only a callsign, class, and section.
- In **Logbook** CSV exports, **Logger** was empty on every row. It now contains the logger's callsign, as ADIF exports do.
- On **Log Contact**, an off-band frequency error stayed on screen after you changed the frequency or cleared the entry with Esc. It now clears with those changes.
- On **Log Contact**, contacts waiting to sync showed the browser's local time, then changed to UTC after syncing. They now show UTC throughout.
- In the W1AW bulletin, radiogram **Filed At (UTC)**, message **Reply Date/Time (UTC)**, and other date-time pickers, pressing **Now** at a minute such as 12:18 silently blocked saving. Saving now works at any minute.
- On **Manage Schedule**, **Add Shift** showed dates such as `2026-10-28 14:00`, while **Create Multiple Shifts** and **Edit Shift** used a `T` between the date and time. All now use the first format.
- On **Guestbook Manager**, the bonus points panel stayed unchanged after verifying, unverifying, or deleting entries until a reload or background poll. It now updates right away.
- On **Site Safety Checklist**, the CPR/AED item read "versed else trained." It now reads "versed or trained," and upgrading corrects existing events too.
- On **Manage Schedule**, editing a shift could lower its capacity below the number already signed up, leaving counts such as "5/1 filled." Saving now refuses that change with a message.
- On **Reports**, **Sections Worked** counted YT, MX, and DX separately during ARRL Field Day, giving a higher count than the dashboard, public page, and section map. It now counts YT as TER and MX as DX, matching those pages.
- In event setup, YT (Yukon) and MX (Mexico) were offered as the event's own section for ARRL Field Day. They are now offered only for Winter Field Day.
- In the ADIF import review table, remapping a section, band, or mode left the original file value on screen even though the import used the mapping. The table now shows the mapped value.
- On logging, station, message, report, dashboard, section map, gallery, equipment, schedule, and other pages, browser tabs showed only the site name. Each page now has its own title.
- In the station form, the radio picker was empty without an explanation when every radio was already the primary radio at another station. The form now explains this and links to adding a radio.
- On **Log Contact**, contacts logged in the same second could appear in the wrong order in the QSO list with their QSO numbers swapped, and the up arrow could recall the earlier contact instead of the latest. The same list problem affected **Transcribe Paper Log** when contacts shared a time, recorded to the minute. Tied contacts now appear in the order they were logged, and the up arrow on **Log Contact** recalls the latest. Stored data and exports were not affected.
- During ARRL Field Day, contacts logged at 1,500 W still got the 2× power multiplier on **Scoring** and the submission sheet, and Cabrillo reported `CATEGORY-POWER: LOW`, because power came from the event and station settings. The multiplier, the submission sheet, and Cabrillo now use the highest power on any contact. The settings count only until the first contact. Contacts above an operating class's power limit (500 W for ARRL Field Day Classes A, B, and C, and 100 W for Winter Field Day) are still logged but score 0 points. **Scoring** warns about them, and **Logbook** marks them **no credit**. **Start Session** also refuses power above the station radio's rating. The event form now shows the class limit under **Maximum Power (Watts)** and won't save a higher power.
- During ARRL Field Day, switching a station to commercial mains removed the emergency power bonus from **Scoring**, but the submission sheet still checked only the event's power sources and listed the bonus, so its bonus lines added up to more than its total. Switching the station back also restored the bonus. Each contact now keeps the power source its station had when it was logged. The sheet's **Power Source** boxes include the stations' sources, its bonus lines match **Scoring**, and a Class A entry with any contact on commercial mains shows as **A-Commercial**. Contacts made on mains keep the emergency power bonus and the 5× multiplier off after the station switches back. Upgrading records each existing contact's power source from its station's current setting.

## 26.10.13 - 2026-10-26

### Added

- On **Log Contact**, **Advanced fields** opens a panel below the exchange box with **Freq (MHz)**, **RST sent**, **RST rcvd**, **Grid**, **Name**, and **Notes**. Alt+A opens or closes it, and this device remembers that choice. With the panel open, Tab from the exchange box moves through the fields in order. Shift+Tab from **Freq (MHz)** returns to the exchange box, Enter logs the contact or saves a recalled edit, and Esc returns to the exchange box. Frequency stays filled between contacts and after a band or mode switch if it belongs on the new band. The other fields clear after each contact. Signal reports accept values such as 59, 599, and -10. Grid squares accept 4 or 6 characters and are stored like FN31pr. The log shows frequency, reports, grid, and name in **Details**, or on the card line on phones. Recalling a contact with the up arrow also loads its advanced fields for editing.
- On **Log Contact**, a frequency on another band blocks logging. For example, entering 7.200 MHz while on 20m shows **Switch to 40m** and focuses the button. Press Enter or click the button to switch and log the contact with the exchange and advanced fields you typed. If validation fails on the new band, your entry stays in the box with an error. Esc goes back without switching.
- On **Log Contact**, band and mode pickers in the session bar let you switch without leaving the logging screen. Mouse or touch selections switch immediately. Alt+B and Alt+M focus the pickers, arrow keys choose a value, Enter switches, and Esc cancels. You can also type a band or mode alone in the exchange box and press Enter: `40M` or `40`, `CW`, `PH` or `SSB`, `DIG`, or a pair such as `20M PH`. A switch ends the current session and starts a new one on the same station with the same power, as **End Session** followed by a new session would. Contacts waiting to sync go with you and still count toward the session where they were logged. A switch is refused while offline, when the station is unavailable, or when another station already uses that band and mode. GOTA stations are exempt from the conflict check. After a switch, the log keeps the most recent 20 contacts from the sessions this one continued from through a chain of band or mode switches. They appear greyed out and read-only above the new session's contacts. Starting a fresh session from **Station Select** or after **End Session** shows an empty log, as before. A divider marks each switch with that session's band, mode, and start time, such as "40m Phone · 21:52". The current session's divider is highlighted. Earlier contacts can't be recalled, edited, or deleted here. Use **Logbook** to edit or delete them. At GOTA stations, **GOTA Operator** carries over through a switch only, not into a fresh session. The pickers replace the hint to end the session to change band or mode.
- ADIF exports include `FREQ`, `RST_SENT`, `RST_RCVD`, `GRIDSQUARE`, `NAME`, and `COMMENT` (notes) when those fields were logged. Cabrillo QSO lines use the logged frequency in kHz below 1 GHz instead of the band edge.
- Contacts from N1MM Logger+, WSJT-X/JTDX, fldigi, generic ADIF over UDP, and ADIF file import keep frequency, sent and received reports, grid square, name, and comment. Frequency, reports, grid, and name appear in **Details**, and the fields are exported like browser-logged contacts. ADIF imports and ADIF-based loggers also preserve other record fields, such as `MY_GRIDSQUARE`, `TX_PWR`, `QSO_DATE_OFF`, `TIME_OFF`, and `APP_` fields, for ADIF export. Values held in Field Day Commander, including edited reports, take precedence over the original record. Reports such as `5NN` that don't fit the app's fields are preserved for export. Merging an ADIF import into an existing contact fills only empty fields.
- Below 1280px wide, the **Log Contact** session bar uses two rows: station name with power beside it, QSOs, and **End Session** on top, then band and mode pickers with the sub-mode box below. At 1280px and wider, it stays in one row.

### Fixed

- While editing a recalled QSO on **Log Contact**, using the up arrow or tapping a QSO, the screen could leave editing mode every 30 seconds or when another queued contact finished syncing. The exchange stayed in the box, so pressing Enter logged a new contact (a dupe) instead of saving the edit. Editing mode now holds until you save, delete, or cancel.
- Since 26.10.12, **Manage Schedule** > **Roles** showed only the edit (pencil) button for each role, so you couldn't delete roles. The delete (trash) button is back and still asks for confirmation, deletes the role's unassigned shifts, and refuses to delete a role with assigned shifts.
- Entering the organization callsign in lowercase, such as w1abc, during setup or in **System Preferences** was rejected as an invalid callsign. It is now saved in uppercase, like operator callsigns. Setup also kept clearing the organization fields and weather email whenever a field failed validation. They now keep what you typed.
- On the Shift Dashboard, the timeline's horizontal scrollbar could sit on top of the bottom role row in browsers with overlay scrollbars, such as Chrome and Edge on Windows 11. It now sits below the chart.
- With GOTA contacts or dupes in the log, QSO counts disagreed between the dashboard, **Scoring**, **Logbook**, and the event dashboard, and **Logbook** included GOTA bonus points in **QSO Points**. QSO totals now count non-duplicate contacts including GOTA, **Total Logged** counts every contact, **Dupes** includes GOTA dupes, and **Logbook** separates GOTA bonus points from **QSO Points** and leaves out contacts the rules give no credit.
- On **Scoring** and the event dashboard, a pending bonus could show as **Claimed** but contribute zero to its summary tile, and **Verified** and **Claimed** showed points while **Unclaimed** counted bonuses. All three tiles now count bonuses, with points or Winter Field Day objective multipliers below **Verified** and **Claimed**, and pending claims show what verification would award in both the tile and the row.
- On phones, the header's **LIVE** / **Ends in** bar appeared after the page loaded and pushed the page down, so a quick tap on **Select Station** or another page could land on the wrong card. The countdown now takes its space from the first paint.
- Reloading a GOTA station's logging screen mid-session left **GOTA Operator** blank even though the operator's name was saved on contacts. The panel now refills from the session's latest contact with operator details.
- Browser confirmation popups for deleting events, stations, equipment, contacts, photos, messages, guestbook entries, and safety checklist items, **End Session** on **Log Contact**, dropping or checking out of a shift, enabling or disabling weather sources, entering or leaving station mode, and removing a manual bonus claim looked different in each browser, and choosing a browser's "don't show more dialogs from this page" option made those actions silently do nothing. These confirmations now open an in-app dialog, a bottom sheet on phones and centered on larger screens, with a button that names the action, such as **End Session** or **Delete**, and is red for destructive actions. **Cancel**, Esc, or tapping outside the dialog cancels, and keyboard focus starts on **Cancel** for destructive actions. Cancelling a confirmation on **Manage Weather** now leaves the toggle switch where it was.

## 26.10.12 - 2026-10-23

### Added

- The guestbook has a kiosk mode for a shared tablet or laptop at the event. Open it from the small Kiosk mode link at the bottom of the guestbook page. It drops the header and menu, shows the form at the top with the latest entries below it, newest first, and after each entry a countdown ring on the thank-you screen clears it for the next visitor. ### Changed

- Role management required **Manage System Settings**. It now uses **Manage Roles**, so you can let someone edit roles without access to system preferences or site branding. With only this permission, they see only **Role Management** in **System Settings**. Upgrades grant it to roles that could manage settings, and the System Administrator role must keep it.
- Equipment menus now use **My Equipment** and **Equipment Catalog** instead of **My Catalog** and **All User Catalogs**, matching the page headings. **Settings** is now **System Settings** in the sidebar, user menu, and page heading. Sign-in and session controls use **Sign In** and **Sign Out**, including station mode's former **End Session** button.

- The unused **Manage Equipment** permission, labeled Legacy, is gone from the Role Manager. Nothing checked it. The narrower equipment permissions control equipment access, and upgrades remove it from any role or user that had it.
- The event and station forms now explain that a methane generator counts as alternate power but should be recorded as **Generator**, since engine-driven generators don't qualify for ARRL's 5× multiplier.

## 26.10.11 - 2026-10-21

### Changed

- Cabrillo and ADIF exports now name the program as Field Day Commander instead of FD Commander. So do the service names in `systemctl status`, the backup timers, and the messages from `deploy.sh`, `update.sh`, `backup.sh`, `restore.sh` and the Docker container. ### Security

- PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) let anyone who could reach the server run code on it without signing in. FD Commander now runs on FrankenPHP 1.13.2, which is built with patched PHP. Update with `update.sh` or pull the new Docker image as soon as you can. Installs also turn off PHP's session upload progress tracking, which FD Commander never used.


### Fixed

- Since 26.10.10, the PDF reports had a white strip across the top of the first page, above the blue title banner. In the ARRL submission sheet and the Winter Field Day event summary, the same strip showed on every later page that starts with a banner. The banners reach the top edge of the page again.

- The equipment owner contact list PDF filled only about three quarters of each page before starting the next, and an owner with several emergency phone numbers could push a row or two onto a page of their own. Pages now fill down to the footer.
- The dashboard widget size menus offered 4 columns, but the dashboard is only 3 columns wide. Picking 4 squeezed in an extra narrow column and shuffled every widget around. The menus now go up to 3, and widgets already set to 4 show as full width.
- While editing a dashboard on a phone or tablet, the widget toolbars slid over the top bar as you scrolled. They now scroll under it.
- The widget resize menu's column labels wrapped onto two lines on phones. They now read 1, 2 and 3, with a note that phones always show one column.
