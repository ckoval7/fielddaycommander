Run the GOTA Station

Set up the Get On The Air station, log each new operator, and earn the GOTA bonus at ARRL Field Day.

How-To Guides › GOTA Station

The GOTA (Get On The Air) station is where new operators make Field Day contacts with a coach beside them. In FD Commander, the Field Day GOTA station is a regular station with a GOTA flag. It sends its own callsign, has its own dupe checking, and gets its own bonus on the Scoring page. This guide covers setting up the GOTA station, logging each operator, and how the bonus is scored.

What You Need

  • A Field Day event in Class A or F. Those are the only classes that allow a GOTA station. Winter Field Day has no GOTA station, so FD Commander won't let you turn it on for a WFD event.
  • A GOTA callsign. The rules require it to be different from your main callsign.
  • A computer at the GOTA station, set up like the others in Set Up Multi-Station Logging.

Step 1: Turn On GOTA for the Event

On the event form, find the GOTA Station (Get On The Air) card. Check Enable GOTA Station, enter the GOTA Callsign, and click Update Event. First Event Setup covers the rest of the form.

Don't skip this step. Without it, GOTA contacts will still be logged but earn no bonus, and the Scoring page will show "GOTA contact(s) logged but no GOTA station is configured" under Corrections & Notices.

Step 2: Mark the GOTA Station

Under Event Management > Stations, add the GOTA station or click Edit on an existing one. In the Station Type card, check GOTA Station (Get On The Air) and save. The station card will show a GOTA badge.

An event can have only one GOTA station, and it can't also be VHF/UHF Only or a Satellite Station.

Step 3: Sign In the Control Operator

Bind the GOTA laptop to the GOTA station the same way as the other stations (see Set Up Station Mode). Once it's bound, you'll see a note that reads "GOTA station: control operator signs in," and the box is labeled Control operator callsign instead of Your callsign.

The licensed control operator, usually your GOTA coach, types their own callsign and clicks Start Logging. The coach stays with the station. GOTA operators don't sign in on this screen. They're entered on the logging screen in Step 5.

If the GOTA laptop isn't in station mode, the coach signs in with their own account instead and picks the GOTA station under Logging > Log Contact. Logging works the same way from there.

Step 4: Start a Supervised Session

The Start Logging Session dialog opens for the GOTA station. Pick the Band, Mode, and Power (watts) as usual. The event form reminds you that GOTA stations must run 100 W or less.

Under GOTA Station Options, turn on Supervised Session (GOTA Coach present) when a coach is supervising, then click Start Session. Only contacts from supervised sessions count toward the coach bonus.

You can't change this setting once the session starts. If a coach arrives partway through, end the session and start a new one with the toggle on.

Step 5: Enter Each GOTA Operator

The logging screen on the GOTA station has a GOTA Operator panel. Its header shows Callsign on air with your GOTA callsign, and Your Exchange at the top uses the GOTA callsign too.

  • For a registered member, type at least two letters of their name or callsign in Search registered user (optional) and pick them from the list. You should see a Linked badge with their name.
  • For a visitor, fill in First Name and Last Name, plus Callsign (optional) if they have one.

Enter the GOTA operator before they make their first contact. FD Commander won't log a GOTA contact with the panel empty. You'll see "Enter the GOTA operator in the GOTA Operator panel before logging." Each contact is credited to whoever is in the panel when it's logged, not to the coach. The operator stays set from one contact to the next. When someone new sits down, click Change next to a linked member, or type over the names.

The names are worth getting right. The Submission Sheet on the Reports page has a GOTA page that lists each GOTA operator with their contacts by mode. A linked member whose profile is marked Youth (age 18 or younger) will also count toward the youth participation bonus.

How the Get On The Air Station Is Scored

  • GOTA QSO Bonus: 5 points for each GOTA contact that isn't a dupe, on any mode. It's added after the power multiplier, so it isn't multiplied, and GOTA contacts don't count toward your QSO points.
  • GOTA Coach Bonus: a single 100-point bonus once at least 10 GOTA contacts (dupes excluded) were logged in supervised sessions. The count covers every supervised session, not one coach's.

FD Commander doesn't cap GOTA contacts or limit how many one operator can make. The coach bonus comes from supervised sessions alone, so confirming a GOTA Coach shift check-in on the schedule won't award it.

On the Scoring page, the QSO Points column shows a GOTA Contacts count once the GOTA station has logged one. Those contacts aren't included in Valid QSOs. The Bonus Points column has a GOTA Station Bonus section with each bonus and its progress, such as "8 supervised contacts (need 10+)." The score equation at the top adds a GOTA Bonus term.

Dupes and the Band and Mode Rule

GOTA contacts are only checked against other GOTA contacts. If the phone station worked K5XSR on 20m, the GOTA station can still work K5XSR on 20m phone and it won't be a dupe.

The GOTA station is also exempt from the one-transmitter-per-band-and-mode rule. It can start a session on a band and mode another station is using, and its sessions won't block the other stations either.

If the GOTA station logs in N1MM Logger+ or WSJT-X, or you import an ADIF file for it, those contacts count as GOTA contacts and are dupe-checked the same way. They aren't tied to a GOTA operator, though, so they won't appear by name on the Submission Sheet's GOTA page. To credit each operator, log GOTA contacts in the browser.
