For the Operator

Take your shift. Work the bands.

Open the built-in logger on a laptop, tablet, or phone. Every contact joins the club's shared log and updates the live score.

  • Catch the dupe before you log it.

    Live duplicate checks look across stations. Exchange validation helps catch mistakes while the contact is still in front of you.

  • Make the next shift easy.

    On a shared computer set up in station mode, operators start their turn with their callsign.

  • Keep the logger you know.

    Built-in UDP listeners receive contacts from N1MM Logger+, WSJT-X, JTDX, and fldigi. No double entry. You can also import an ADIF file.

  • Keep logging through a connection drop.

    If the browser loses contact with the server, built-in logging queues contacts locally and syncs them when the connection returns.

Quick Answers

Will it fit our club?

All questions →
Does it need internet?
No. Once installed, Field Day Commander runs on your local network without internet. Logging, scoring, scheduling, and bonus tracking all work offline. Internet is only needed for optional features like live weather updates and email.
Does it work with N1MM Logger+ or WSJT-X?
Yes. Keep the software your operators know. UDP listeners bring contacts from N1MM Logger+, WSJT-X, JTDX, and fldigi into the shared log.
Does it handle Winter Field Day?
Yes, set the event type to Winter Field Day and the scoring, objectives, and Cabrillo log follow the WFDA rules.
What does it cost?
Nothing. It's free and open source under the GPL v3, with no paid tiers.

Set up your club's server

Run these commands on your server. Replace YOUR_SERVER_HOSTNAME with a hostname your station devices can resolve. For a Pi named fd-commander, that may be fd-commander.local; verify it on your network first. Choose your server address.

  1. Clone git clone https://github.com/ckoval7/fielddaycommander.git
  2. Enter cd fielddaycommander
  3. Deploy sudo ./deploy.sh --domain YOUR_SERVER_HOSTNAME
