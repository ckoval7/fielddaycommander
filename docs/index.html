How It Works

From setup to submission.

The features you'll use at each stage of a Field Day event.

All features →

  1. Deploy

    once

    Install it on a Linux machine on your LAN.

    • Runs on your LAN

      Any Linux box or Raspberry Pi, no internet.

    • Deploy script or Docker

      One command, or Docker Compose.

    • Backups on a schedule

      Hourly database, nightly full backup.

  2. Plan

    weeks out

    Set up stations, people, and gear before the weekend.

    • Volunteer scheduling

      Operators sign up, and you see the gaps.

    • Equipment management

      Track whose gear goes to which station.

    • Rules and safety checklists

      ARRL Field Day and Winter Field Day, built in.

  3. Operate

    24 hours

    Log from every station while the score updates live.

    • Multi-station logging

      Browser, N1MM Logger+, WSJT-X, or fldigi, dupe-checked.

    • Station computers or your own

      Operators take turns by typing their callsign.

    • Live score and section map

      Both update with every contact.

    • Bonus tracking

      Log NTS traffic and sign in visitors.

  4. Submit

    after

    Get your entry ready for the ARRL or the WFDA.

    • Cabrillo export

      A clean log file, ready to upload.

    • Entry form summary

      Your score, bonuses, and objectives laid out.

    • ADIF export

      Load the weekend into the club's logging software.

Quick Answers

The usual questions.

All questions →
Does it need internet?
Only to install it, and then it runs entirely on your LAN.
Does it work with N1MM Logger+ or WSJT-X?
Yes, built-in UDP listeners bring in contacts from N1MM Logger+, WSJT-X, JTDX, and fldigi.
Does it handle Winter Field Day?
Yes, set the event type to Winter Field Day and the scoring, objectives, and Cabrillo log follow the WFDA rules.
What does it cost?
Nothing. It's free and open source under the GPL v3, with no paid tiers.
