What Sets It Apart

The whole event in one place.

Most Field Day software is a logger. Field Day Commander also keeps track of the club's gear, the operating schedule, and the weather.

  1. Track every piece of gear

    setup to teardown

    Members add their radios, antennas, and other gear to their own catalog, then commit what they're bringing to the event. You can assign each item to a station and mark it delivered when it shows up. Owners get an email when their gear is delivered and again when it's returned. On Sunday, the return checklist lists every item under its owner, so it all goes home with the right person.

    • ✓Club gear and members' gear in one inventory
    • ✓Gear assigned to each station
    • ✓Printable delivery and return checklists

  2. Fill every shift

    weeks out

    Lay out the operating schedule and let operators sign up for open shifts. The shift dashboard shows which hours still need someone, so you can find a volunteer for the overnight before the weekend instead of during it. The safety checklist for your event is in the same place, ready to check off as the site comes together.

    • ✓Shift sign-up with coverage gaps shown
    • ✓Operator, GOTA coach, and safety officer roles
    • ✓The safety checklist for your event

  3. Warn the site about storms

    live

    When the server has an internet connection, Field Day Commander checks the National Weather Service for storm warnings at your site. Any warning shows up in a banner at the top of every screen, including the logging screens. You can also post a lightning alert yourself, with instructions to stop operating or take shelter. Members who opt in get alerts by email too.

    • ✓Storm warnings from the National Weather Service
    • ✓Lightning alerts you post from the site
    • ✓A banner on every screen

  3. Operate

    24 hours

    Every station logs to one shared log, and the score updates live.

    • Multi-station logging

      Browser, N1MM Logger+, WSJT-X, or fldigi, dupe-checked.

      Operators take turns by typing their callsign.

    • Live score and section map

      Both update with every contact.

      Log NTS traffic and sign in visitors.

    after

One Team

Operate as one team.

From the person who set up the server to the operator in the 2 AM chair, everyone works from the same event, the same schedule, and the same log. Field Day Commander ships with four default roles, and you can change every permission to fit how your club runs.

  • Admin · deploys and configures

    whole server

    Gets the server running, creates accounts, and handles the infrastructure so everyone else can focus on the event.

  • Event Manager · plans and coordinates

    the event

    Builds the schedule, assigns stations, runs the safety checklist, and handles the Cabrillo export at the end.

  • Station Captain · runs a station

    one station

    Tracks the station's gear, coordinates band and mode use, and sets up station mode on its computer.

  • Operator · logs contacts

    the log

    Opens a browser and starts logging, with instant dupe checks and live band activity.

Quick Answers

The usual questions.

All questions →
Does it need internet?
Only to install it, and then it runs entirely on your LAN.
Does it work with N1MM Logger+ or WSJT-X?
Yes, built-in UDP listeners bring in contacts from N1MM Logger+, WSJT-X, JTDX, and fldigi.
Does it handle Winter Field Day?
Yes, set the event type to Winter Field Day and the scoring, objectives, and Cabrillo log follow the WFDA rules.
What does it cost?
Nothing. It's free and open source under the GPL v3, with no paid tiers.
